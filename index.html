Artificial Intelligence

AI That Works For Farmers

Transparent, explainable AI recommendations — not a black box. Every suggestion comes with a clear reason.

Smart Procurement Matching

AI scores every farmer-factory pair using distance, price, quality, quantity and reliability — and explains the score.

Price Forecasting

Seasonal price trend analysis helps farmers decide the best time to sell and factories plan procurement budgets.

Supplier Reliability Score

Every farmer gets a 0–100 reliability score based on delivery history, quality, timeliness and consistency.

Harvest Prediction

Predict harvest windows based on crop type and sowing date so factories can plan procurement in advance.

Surplus & Shortage Detection

Real-time analysis of supply vs demand across districts to alert government and FPOs before crises occur.

Route Optimization

Free routing via OSRM calculates the fastest, most cost-effective delivery routes for logistics providers.
